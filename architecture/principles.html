Driving Principles

The rules that decide every design call — from architecture to production sign-off.

1Think
Altitude before build
  • System thinkingOptimise the whole, not the part
  • Platform thinkingBuild for the tenth team, not the first
  • Data & AI is softwareSame engineering discipline, no exemptions
  • Simplicity as a constraintComplexity has to be earned
2Compose
Build once, reuse everywhere
  • Lego blocksSmall, replaceable capabilities
  • Contracts over couplingInterfaces, not integrations
  • Golden pathBest practice is the default path
  • Self-serveTeams consume without waiting on us
3Execute
Deterministic first
  • Deterministic by defaultRules, policies, validations, gates
  • Agentic by exceptionLLM only where judgement is required
  • Reproducible & idempotentSame input, same result, every time
  • Human accountable at the boundaryAgents assemble evidence — a person signs
4Operate & Assure
Trusted in production
  • Cross-cutting is first classQuality, security, cost, observability designed in
  • Design for failureSmall blast radius, always reversible
  • Fail fast, fail visiblyBreak at the gate, never in production
  • Self-explanatoryReadable by humans and by agents
  • Evidence by constructionAudit is an output, not a phase

Deterministic by default  ·  Agentic by exception  ·  Reusable by design

Every capability we ship is judged against these four columns before it reaches production.
