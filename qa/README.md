# Interview Q&A

Prepared question-and-answer sets, one folder per topic. Each topic has:

- **`framework.md`** — the structured thinking: the argument, the tables, the diagrams.
  This is what she should *understand*.
- **`questions.md`** — likely panel questions with spoken answers and follow-up drills.
  This is what she should *rehearse*.

Answers are anchored to her own artefacts — the North Star platform, the Ops Gate agent,
CostAgent — rather than to generic theory, so every one of them carries the three
cross-cutting signals from [../evaluation-criteria.md](../evaluation-criteria.md):
**ownership · reuse · a measurable outcome**.

## Convention for numbers

Bracketed placeholders like `[N]`, `[X%]` are deliberate — they mark where a real figure
from the evidence bank is needed (open item 6 in [../plan.md](../plan.md)). **Do not go
into the room with brackets unfilled.** An answer with a real number lands at the grade
bar; the same answer without one reads as theory.
