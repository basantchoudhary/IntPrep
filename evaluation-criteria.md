# Senior Solution Engineer — Evaluation Criteria

Role assessed against three technical themes (T1–T3). Each theme has the capability
description followed by the **"At this grade"** bar — the level of evidence expected
for Senior Solution Engineer.

---

## T1 — Solution architecture and enterprise integration

Ownership of technical solution design for complex AI integrations — from requirements
through detailed architecture, component selection, and integration specification —
designing for resilience, failure modes, and confident downstream delivery.

**At this grade**

Owned solution architecture that became the reference design for a domain or programme;
the integration pattern or architecture approach was reused across multiple complex
engagements with measurable reduction in integration risk or rework.

---

## T2 — Deployment readiness, technical assurance and regulatory alignment

Leading technical due diligence and deployment readiness assessments for complex
deployments — surfacing integration, security, data, and regulatory risk before build
begins, and providing technical assurance through delivery and production support.

**At this grade**

Set the technical assurance or deployment readiness bar for a domain — for example an
assurance checklist, risk framework, or regulatory-alignment approach now reused by
other engagements — with measurable reduction in production risk or rework.

---

## T3 — Stakeholder engagement, technical leadership and pattern reuse

Representing EAI's technical architecture credibly across engineering, enterprise
architecture, security, and business partner audiences — building trusted
relationships, converting deployment experience into reusable patterns and standards,
and mentoring junior engineers.

**At this grade**

Recognised as a go-to technical authority by enterprise architecture, security, or
business technology partners across GSK; multiple reusable patterns.

---

## Cross-cutting signals

Three phrases recur across all three themes and should shape every prepared answer:

| Signal | What the panel is listening for |
|---|---|
| **Ownership** | She drove the design/assessment, not just contributed to it |
| **Reuse** | The artefact outlived the engagement and was adopted elsewhere |
| **Measurable outcome** | A number: reduced integration risk, rework, production incidents, cycle time |

Answer shape to default to: **Situation → the design/assessment she owned → how it was
turned into a reusable pattern → who else adopted it → the measured delta.**
